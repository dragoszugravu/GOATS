# Product Mission (Lite)

GOATS (Ultra-Lean AWS) is a serverless roster web app that helps admins publish player updates in under two minutes and helps fans browse a fast, position-grouped roster by providing a cost-optimized AWS stack with an admin console, presigned S3 uploads, and CloudFront delivery.

GOATS serves team administrators who need secure, low-friction publishing and fans who expect fast mobile performance. Unlike heavier stacks, GOATS is fully serverless (S3, CloudFront, DynamoDB, Lambda, Cognito) with least-privilege OIDC CI/CD, targeting p95 LCP under 2.5s and low single-digit monthly spend at light traffic.
